Many is ManyBot's mascot, and also ManyBot's official bot, addable on WhatsApp via the
number **+55 16 99459-1903**.

Using the official bot is completely free and unlimited, and it was designed for people who just want to make
stickers and have some tools directly on WhatsApp without paying a lot for closed bots.

It's hosted on an Ubuntu Server, with 2 vCPU and 4GB of RAM.

# Can I add Many to a group?

Technically, yes. In practice, though, you should keep in mind that it won't have
all the freedom you'd get with the framework.
